# ROOMSHIFT / 格间

## Route

- Branch: `mixed`. Brand identity is approved locally first, then applied to a consumer renovation decision product.
- Brand mode: visual identity with a small application family. Product mode: commerce/consumer plus a high-consideration service flow.
- Audience: Chinese urban households comparing a small-home renovation before speaking with a designer.
- Core job: compare a representative spatial direction, choose household/material/storage constraints, understand a clearly labelled demo budget and produce a recoverable next step.
- Locale: `zh-CN` is primary. English names functional artifacts and brand applications; controls are not duplicated line by line.
- Visual lineage: architectural editorial precision. A consumer-service counterweight keeps configuration language familiar. No exterior brand skin is copied.

## Identity Contract

- The original mark uses two offset wall forms around a vermilion change zone. It is not a UI icon, stock pictogram or legal-clearance claim.
- Primary palette: paper `#f4f5f1`, ink `#171b19`, mineral green `#28584a`, vermilion `#d65337` and structural gray.
- Mineral green means trust and primary action. Vermilion marks a changed area or visual decision and is not reused for generic decoration.
- Primary UI family is a full-CJK system sans stack. Song/Ming system serif is limited to brand display and editorial application titles. Letter spacing remains `0`.
- Mark variants: color and monochrome SVG. Test targets: 16, 24 and 48px, light field and reversed via monochrome treatment.
- Safe space starts at one central red-square width. Do not stretch, rotate, recolor arbitrarily, add a shadow, or replace it with a Lucide icon.

## Product Contract

- The first viewport is the real planner: space proof on the left, household/material/storage decisions on the right.
- The visual stage owns image comparison; the inspector owns configuration and estimate state. No Canvas is used.
- Primary path: compare -> choose household -> choose material -> tune storage -> review budget/time -> export or book review.
- Every changed configuration is undoable; reset restores the baseline. Booking validation preserves entered values and names the recovery action.
- The generated text file is a real local browser download and is explicitly labelled as a concept, not a quote or construction document.
- Desktop keeps the visual proof persistent. Mobile stacks proof before configuration and expands segmented controls for touch.

## Do / Don't

- Do let real space imagery prove the object and let DOM controls carry the interaction.
- Do keep budget, time, units and concept disclaimers visible at the decision point.
- Do use one clear boundary between the visual stage and control inspector.
- Don't nest cards, bake text into images, claim a real transformation, or use visual mood as evidence.
- Don't use Canvas, gradients as decoration, arbitrary rounded containers, or color-only state.

## Acceptance

- Brand: first-glance recognition, color/mono and 16/24/48px mark tests, sample-kit and report applications, transparent source/license record.
- Product: scenario/material/storage updates, before/after range, honest estimate, undo/reset, download, booking validation and success feedback.
- Responsive: 375, 768, 1024 and 1440px; 44px touch targets, visible focus, no clipped controls, reduced motion and readable photo fallbacks.
- Truth: every price, duration and outcome is labelled as demo or concept data; no endorsement, performance claim or real client project is implied.
