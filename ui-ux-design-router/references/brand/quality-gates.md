# Brand And Graphic Quality Gates

Use before presentation and again before final delivery. Record pass, fail, not applicable, owner, and evidence for consequential checks. Do not emit a numeric score unless the user explicitly requests review, audit, scoring, acceptance, or comparison; for those requests, run these gates first and then read [review-scorecard.md](review-scorecard.md).

## Always-On Release Decision

Any applicable failure means `NOT PASS` and cannot be offset by visual polish or a high score:

- the real audience cannot identify the core message, offer, identity, or required response;
- overlap, clipping, unsafe crop, illegible reproduction, or production geometry hides critical content or makes a required deliverable unusable;
- text, essential graphics, controls, or wayfinding fail applicable contrast and legibility;
- an interactive application lacks visible keyboard focus or has undersized required touch targets;
- an interactive or variable application lacks an applicable loading, empty, success, error, or recovery state;
- a high-risk action fails to identify the object and consequence;
- a logo is misused or a required font, image, mark, template, or other asset has unknown rights;
- data, customer marks, endorsements, metrics, reviews, certifications, product claims, or documentary imagery are false, placeholder, or unsupported.

Mark a gate not applicable only when the state or capability cannot occur in the reviewed scope, and record the reason. For an explicit acceptance review, [review-scorecard.md](review-scorecard.md) defines scoring, evidence ceilings, modification order, retests, and stopping conditions.

## Capability Closure Audit

Classify these families as `active` or `not applicable`: brief/message, hierarchy/composition, canvas/modules, typography/verbal identity, color, logo/identity, icons/imagery/data/motion, channel adaptation, rights/accessibility, production, templates/governance, and validation. For every active family, record what triggered it, the chosen rule and reason, the constrained or simplified fallback, and the actual-use proof. Reopen any family missing one of those four answers. Mark `not applicable` only when it cannot occur in the selected mode, system depth and deliverables; this is a relevance filter, not a demand to add every brand layer.

## Brief And Strategy

- The audience, communication objective, desired response, message hierarchy, channels and success criteria are explicit.
- Facts, hypotheses, stakeholder preferences, mandatory claims and approvals are distinguishable.
- The concept can be explained from audience/category/brand evidence without relying on trend words.
- Scope matches the selected depth: artifact, campaign, identity system or governed brand system.

## Visual Fundamentals

- Reading order matches content priority at overview, thumbnail and actual size.
- Contrast, scale, proximity, whitespace, rhythm and figure/ground create one controlled hierarchy.
- Grid, margins, keylines, spacing and type roles repeat coherently; exceptions are optical or meaningful, not accidental.
- Canvas, bands, frames, panels, lockups, image fields and reusable modules each have a communication or production purpose; containment is minimal and nested boxes do not substitute for hierarchy.
- Color roles work in grayscale and for relevant color-vision/contrast needs; no information depends on hue alone.
- Type is routed for its actual logo, campaign, distance, editorial, packaging, signage, social, template, data and reproduction context, and passes language coverage, mixed-text, long/short content, punctuation, numerals, legal copy, fallback and licensing checks.
- Reusable modules define required/optional fields, safe replacement zones, overflow/crop behavior, protected brand regions, named variants and an owner; a difficult replacement-content case passes without one-off repair.
- Fine detail, strokes, corners, shadows, gradients and textures survive the final scale and medium.

## Brand And Logo

- The identity traces to a defensible position and does not rely on a logo alone.
- Existing brand assets are approved; new marks are original, vector, optically refined and not made from emoji, Lucide, Unicode, stock pictograms or generic app icons.
- The mark works in black, monochrome/reversed, small sizes, poor reproduction, common backgrounds and relevant lockups.
- Clear space, minimum size, alignment, variants, co-branding, misuse and accessible naming are defined where needed.
- Name/mark similarity and target-language/cultural risks are screened; legal availability is not claimed without qualified clearance.

## Images, Icons, And Content

- Every image, illustration, icon, pictogram, chart, texture and motion treatment has a communication role and follows one family grammar.
- Sources, licenses, releases, edit rights and generated-media provenance are recorded.
- Claims, customer marks, certifications, product views, testimonials, data and documentary imagery are truthful and attributable.
- Inclusive representation and cultural meaning were reviewed at a depth proportional to the audience and risk.

## Channel And Production

- Current official platform or vendor specifications were obtained; remembered dimensions and generic print defaults were not treated as facts.
- Real content was tested in all required sizes, crops, safe zones, languages, variants and templates.
- Format families preserve message order and brand anchors while adapting composition to distance, aspect, panel and platform; they are not mechanically scaled copies of one master.
- Digital files pass format, profile, resolution/density, transparency, compression, size, alt/caption and fallback checks.
- Print files pass links/fonts, profile/output intent, separations, spot/overprint, image resolution, trim/bleed/safe area, dieline and PDF preflight checks required by the vendor.
- Packaging codes and legal content are owner-approved and physically verified; signage is checked at distance/location; templates survive ordinary user edits.
- Source masters, finals, previews and manifest are organized, versioned and free of unexplained duplicates.

## Validation

- Compare at least one representative application and one difficult edge case, not only the hero mockup.
- Use blur/squint, grayscale, thumbnail, actual-size and extreme-size checks.
- Separate behavioral/comprehension testing from aesthetic preference; ask perception questions after task/message tests when order could bias results.
- Record what changed after critique or testing and why. Stop polishing when the acceptance criteria pass and remaining changes are unsupported preference.

## Automatic Rejection

Reject work that:

- decorates before establishing message hierarchy;
- copies a protected mark, proprietary asset, exact composition or living designer's signature style;
- presents AI output, mockups, invented claims or placeholder data as real evidence;
- uses an icon or emoji as a logo substitute;
- creates inaccessible or illegible content for aesthetic effect;
- ignores the printer, fabricator, platform, legal owner or licensing requirements;
- delivers only a beautiful mockup with no usable master or production file.
